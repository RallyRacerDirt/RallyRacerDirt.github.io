Paint Hit

How to Play Paint Hit

Tap to shoot paint balls onto a rotating cylindrical tower. Each hit paints a sector. Splatter enough paint to complete the tower without hitting painted sectors.

Paint Hit is a thrilling arcade target puzzle. Shoot bright paintballs at spinning circular towers, covering every segment in paint while avoiding your own splatters.

Controls Guide:

  • Left Click or Tap: Shoot paintball at rotating tower

Gameplay Tips:

  • Patience is key: observe rotation speed and pause during speed shifts.
  • Count your remaining ammo against available unpainted sectors on the wheel.
  • Aim for evenly spaced splatters to give yourself wider landing targets.
