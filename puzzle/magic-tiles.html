Magic Tiles

How to Play Magic Tiles

Black piano tiles stream down the screen in time with popular songs. Tap each black tile as it passes your strike line, maintaining perfect rhythm and tempo.

Magic Tiles is a fast-paced musical rhythm puzzle. Tap descending black piano keys to beloved melodies with perfect timing and without missing a beat.

Controls Guide:

  • Mouse Click or Keyboard (1-4): Tap piano tiles as they descend

Gameplay Tips:

  • Maintain a relaxed posture and focus on the auditory beat of the song.
  • Do not tap empty spaces or white tiles, which triggers an instant game over.
  • Anticipate long hold tiles and keep pressing until the trail completes.
