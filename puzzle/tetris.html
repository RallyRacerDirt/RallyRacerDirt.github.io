Tetris

How to Play Tetris

Rotate and place seven geometric shapes falling from above. Completing a horizontal line without gaps clears it from the matrix. Survive as speed increases!

Tetris is the legendary, universally beloved block-stacking puzzle. Guide falling tetrominoes, arrange gapless horizontal lines, and test your endurance as speeds climb.

Controls Guide:

  • Left or Right Arrow: Move piece
  • Up Arrow: Rotate clockwise
  • Down Arrow: Soft drop
  • Spacebar: Hard drop

Gameplay Tips:

  • Keep an open vertical column on the right edge to set up 4-line Tetris clears.
  • Use the ghost piece guide at the bottom of the matrix to line up exact drops.
  • Do not panic when the stack gets tall; prioritize quick 1-line clears to recover.
