Tetris N Blox

How to Play Tetris N Blox

Drop colorful blocks into neat arrangements, clear horizontal rows, and manage the hold piece reserve to maintain board control under escalating speeds.

Tetris N Blox is a sleek, modern iteration of the classic tetromino puzzle. Enjoy ultra-responsive block controls, vibrant color palettes, and intense puzzle action.

Controls Guide:

  • Arrow Keys: Move and rotate tetrominoes
  • Spacebar: Hard drop piece
  • C Key: Store current piece in hold slot

Gameplay Tips:

  • Store straight I-bars in the hold slot until you are ready for a 4-line clear.
  • Monitor the next piece preview window to plan two moves in advance.
  • Slide pieces into place during the lock delay window for tight placements.
