Paint Blob Ball

How to Play Paint Blob Ball

Swipe or press direction keys to propel the paint blob ball through maze corridors. The ball rolls until colliding with a wall, leaving a trail of bright paint.

Paint Blob Ball is a vibrant 3D maze puzzle. Roll a bouncy paint blob across labyrinth pathways, splashing colorful paint over every floor tile to clear levels.

Controls Guide:

  • Arrow Keys, WASD, or Swipe: Roll the paint blob ball

Gameplay Tips:

  • Since the ball only stops at walls, plan multi-step routes to reach dead ends.
  • Look for closed loop pathways to paint efficiently without backtracking.
  • Cover 100% of white floor tiles to trigger the victory portal.
