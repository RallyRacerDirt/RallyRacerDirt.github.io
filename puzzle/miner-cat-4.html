Miner Cat 4

How to Play Miner Cat 4

Drill deep underground through various strata of soil, rock, and ore. Collect valuable minerals, avoid underground hazards, and return to surface shops for upgrades.

Miner Cat 4 follows an intrepid feline explorer mining deep into mysterious underground caverns in search of valuable gems, ores, and ancient relics.

Controls Guide:

  • WASD or Arrow Keys: Move and navigate tunnels
  • Mouse Click: Dig and swing pickaxe towards cursor

Gameplay Tips:

  • Upgrade drill speed and backpack storage capacity as top priorities.
  • Watch oxygen reserves carefully when venturing deep into lower layers.
  • Light your tunnels with markers so you can find your way back to the elevator.
