Fish Eat

How to Play Fish Eat

Swim through dynamic underwater depths consuming fish smaller than you. With each bite you gain mass and level up, allowing you to prey on increasingly larger sea creatures.

Fish Eat is an exciting underwater survival puzzle. Devour smaller marine creatures to grow larger while avoiding massive predators in the ocean depths.

Controls Guide:

  • WASD, Arrow Keys, or Mouse: Swim and navigate through the water

Gameplay Tips:

  • Always target fish strictly smaller than your current number or size.
  • Steer clear of open water when giant predator shadows appear.
  • Corner schools of tiny fish against coral reefs for quick mass gains.
