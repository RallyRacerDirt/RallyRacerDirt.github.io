Melon Playground

How to Play Melon Playground

Spawn melon ragdolls and various mechanical gadgets. Connect parts using ropes, springs, and gears to construct automated contraptions and test wacky physics interactions.

Melon Playground is a creative physics sandbox puzzle game. Experiment with ragdoll physics, mechanical contraptions, weapons, and interactive elements.

Controls Guide:

  • Mouse Drag: Spawn, drag, and position objects
  • Spacebar: Toggle physics simulation pause and resume
  • Right Click: Open object context menu

Gameplay Tips:

  • Use the pause function to assemble intricate mechanical setups safely.
  • Experiment with combining thrusters and floating balloons for aerial creations.
  • Save your favorite contraption blueprints to quickly respawn them anytime.
