Mahjong Quest

How to Play Mahjong Quest

Search the multi-layered board for pairs of identical tiles that are free (having at least one open side and no tiles resting directly on top).

Mahjong Quest is an epic tile-matching solitaire puzzle. Pair matching unblocked Mahjong tiles to dismantle ancient layered structures.

Controls Guide:

  • Mouse Click or Tap: Select two matching exposed tiles

Gameplay Tips:

  • Prioritize matching tiles from tall stacks to reveal more hidden tiles.
  • Keep board balance by working on both sides rather than clearing one corner completely.
  • Use the shuffle power-up strategically when valid moves run low.
