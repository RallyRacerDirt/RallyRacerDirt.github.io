How to Play Vex 8

Push your skills to the brink in Vex 8! The newest entry in the world-famous platforming saga brings innovative trap mechanics, secret pathways, and endless challenge modes.

Encounter red-light green-light stealth zones, reverse conveyor belts, disappearing platforms, and high-velocity jump pads. Explore each Act to locate secret golden keys that unlock hidden bonus levels.

Featuring silky 60fps performance and instantaneous respawns, Vex 8 is pure, unadulterated platforming perfection!

Controls Guide:

  • Move / Jump: WASD or Arrow Keys
  • Slide: Hold S / Down while running
  • Restart: R Key

Gameplay Tips:

  • In Red-Light zones, freeze all movement immediately when the sensor turns red to avoid instant vaporization!
  • Look for subtle cracks in walls that indicate secret passageways and bonus coins.
  • Chain wall-jumps between narrow shafts to quickly ascend high industrial towers.
  • Practice the infinite challenge mode to sharpen your reflex responses.
