How to Play Snail Bob 4: Space

Leave Earth behind in Snail Bob 4: Space! Our adventurous snail friend dons a high-tech spacesuit and blasts into the cosmos to undertake an interstellar rescue mission.

Experience zero-gravity physics, reverse gravity fields, laser barriers, and extraterrestrial creatures. Float through orbit, activate magnetic rails, and steer Bob safely past spinning space satellite gears.

With stellar cosmic environments and mind-bending orbital puzzles, Snail Bob 4 is an out-of-this-world adventure!

Controls Guide:

  • Mouse / Tap: Click switches, zero-G thrusters, and space machinery.
  • Spacebar: Stop moving / retract into pressurized shell.
  • Gravity Inverter: Click to flip ceiling and floor gravity.
