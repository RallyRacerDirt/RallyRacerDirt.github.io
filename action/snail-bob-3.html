How to Play Snail Bob 3

Get ready for an Egyptian odyssey in Snail Bob 3! While visiting a historical museum, Bob touches a magical time-portal sarcophagus and is whisked away to Ancient Egypt.

Help Bob navigate scorching desert dunes, pyramid burial chambers, and hidden pharaoh tombs. Face off against laser-reflecting mirrors, rolling boulders, sarcophagus mummies, and ancient booby traps.

Combine brains and timing to bring Snail Bob back safely to the modern era!

Controls Guide:

  • Mouse / Touch: Click to interact with Egyptian mechanisms, switches, and mirrors.
  • Spacebar: Stop Bob and hide in shell.
  • Speed Button: Double Bob's crawling speed.

Gameplay Tips:

  • Direct ancient light beams onto pyramid sun-sensors to lower heavy stone gates.
  • Hide in Bob's shell to wait out rolling boulders or passing mummy beetles.

  • Use anti-gravity platforms to float across bottomless sandy chasms.
