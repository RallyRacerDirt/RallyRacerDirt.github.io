How to Play Vex 7

The action reaches unprecedented heights in Vex 7! For the first time in the series, face off against hostile robotic security guards and airborne drones while navigating lethal obstacle courses.

Pick up swords and laser blades to slice through enemies, parry projectile attacks, and shatter glass barriers. Combine acrobatic parkour slides with martial arts combat to overcome 10 thrilling Acts.

Unlock stylish skins, conquer the grueling Tower of Terror, and claim your place as the ultimate Vex champion!

Controls Guide:

  • Move & Jump: WASD / Arrow Keys
  • Slide & Crouch: S / Down Arrow
  • Attack / Strike: Spacebar (when weapon is equipped)

Gameplay Tips:

  • Time your sword strikes to deflect incoming enemy drone missiles back at attackers.
  • Stay alert when jumping on collapsing platforms that drop into electrified water.
  • Equip shields before entering crowded drone chambers.
  • Use the grapple anchors to swing across wide hazardous chasms.
