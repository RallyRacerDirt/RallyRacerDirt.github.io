How to Play Vex 4

The stakes are higher and the obstacles deadlier in Vex 4! Guide your fearless stickman hero through 9 intricate Acts, each featuring a grueling Hard Mode counterpart for true platforming masters.

Face off against moving laser beams, crumbling terrain, high-velocity bouncy pads, and swimming labyrinths. Test your stamina in the 30-floor Challenge Room where mistakes mean starting over!

Earn trophies, unlock stars, and prove your parkour reflexes in Vex 4!

Controls Guide:

  • Move: A / D or Left / Right Arrows
  • Jump: W or Up Arrow
  • Crouch / Slide: S or Down Arrow
  • Restart Checkpoint: R Key

  • Don't stand on purple crumbling blocks for more than a second or they will collapse!
  • Orange bounce blocks send you skyward; hold the jump key to maximize your launch height.
  • Use the R key to instantly respawn at your last checkpoint if you miss a speedrun split.
