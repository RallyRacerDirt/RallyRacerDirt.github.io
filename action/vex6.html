How to Play Vex 6

Vex 6 delivers the smoothest, most responsive stickman parkour yet! Featuring 9 brand new Acts, 9 Hard Mode variations, and an exciting Daily Bonus stage that changes every single day.

Earn coins by completing levels and finishing daily tasks. Use your earnings to unlock over 60 awesome character skins and rare trail effects. Navigate spinning maces, spring traps, and multi-directional laser grids.

Vex 6 is an essential thrill-ride for fans of fast-paced precision platforming!

Controls Guide:

  • Move / Jump: WASD or Arrow Keys
  • Slide / Down: S or Down Arrow
  • Pause: Esc or P

Gameplay Tips:

  • Collect coins scattered across levels to unlock rare stickman cosmetics in the shop.
  • Check the Daily Tasks board for bonus coin bounties and free skin rewards.
  • Use momentum from down-slopes to launch into super-long aerial slides.
  • Watch the shadow indicators on laser cannons to predict when beams will fire.
