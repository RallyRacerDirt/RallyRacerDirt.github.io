How to Play Vex Hyper Dash

Feel the pure rush of speed in Vex Hyper Dash! This high-octane spin-off blends classic Vex parkour with supersonic running action.

Chain dashes, leap across dizzying chasms, and slide under laser beams without losing a shred of momentum. Every fraction of a second counts as you sprint through neon obstacle courses designed for speed demons.

Test your reflexes, set blistering world records, and become an unstoppable force in Vex Hyper Dash!

Controls Guide:

  • Jump: W / Up Arrow / Spacebar
  • Hyper Dash: D / Right Arrow (Tap to boost forward)
  • Slide: S / Down Arrow
