Snail Bob 5: Love Story

How to Play Snail Bob 5: Love Story

Love is in the air in Snail Bob 5: Love Story! After falling head-over-heels for a famous snail singing star, Bob embarks on a whimsical, romantic quest through the enchanted woodland to meet her.

Overcome tricky obstacles including sneaky chameleon lizards, snapping traps, bouncy spider webs, and flowing rivers. Use clever contraptions, tree elevators, and friendly forest bugs to assist Bob on his romantic journey.

Enjoy charming love-themed storytelling, delightful visuals, and 25 clever puzzle levels!

Controls Guide:

  • Mouse Click: Manipulate forest objects, ropes, and levers.
  • Spacebar: Stop Bob in his tracks.
  • Turn Button: Change Bob's crawling direction.

Gameplay Tips:

  • Use spider webs to bounce Bob up onto higher tree branches safely.
  • Feed flies to hungry frogs so they stick their tongues out to form convenient bridges.
  • Collect all 3 love-heart stars hidden in foliage, flowers, and tree hollows.
  • Don't let Bob crawl too close to prickly hedgehogs or hot campfires!
