How to Play Vex X3M

The ultimate crossover has arrived in Vex X3M! Combine the adrenaline-pumping motorcycle stunts of Moto X3M with the lethal, razor-sharp obstacle courses of the Vex universe.

Strap on your helmet, rev your dirt bike, and speed through loop-de-loops, spinning saws, crumbling platforms, and explosive spike pits. Perform daring front and back flips in mid-air to shave vital seconds off your race time.

Conquer 30 intense stunt stages and show off your two-wheeled daredevil skills!

Controls Guide:

  • Accelerate: W or Up Arrow
  • Brake / Reverse: S or Down Arrow
  • Lean Back / Front: A / D or Left / Right Arrows
  • Restart: R Key

Gameplay Tips:

  • Perform 360-degree front and back flips to reduce your race timer by 0.5 seconds per flip!
  • Always land with both tires parallel to the surface to avoid wiping out.
  • Ease off the throttle when approaching vertical loop-de-loops to maintain grip.
  • Touch checkpoint poles with your bike frame to lock in your stage progress.
