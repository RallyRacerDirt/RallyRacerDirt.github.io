How to Play Vex X3M 2

Rev up for even wilder stunt racing in Vex X3M 2! Return to the saddle with more powerful bikes, insane track layouts, and jaw-dropping hazard combinations.

Race across giant gears, jump over laser grids, blast through dynamite barrels, and survive dizzying drops. Unlock futuristic motorcycles, quad bikes, and customize your rider with radical helmets.

Master track physics, stick every landing, and claim 3-star ratings across all stages!

Controls Guide:

  • Drive: W / Up Arrow to speed up, S / Down to brake
  • Tilt / Balance: A / D or Left / Right Arrows
  • Nitro Boost: Spacebar (when filled)

Gameplay Tips:

  • Save your Nitro boost for steep uphill climbs and long chasm jumps.
  • Control your bike angle in mid-air to stick landings on angled downhill ramps cleanly.
