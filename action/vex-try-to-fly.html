Vex Try to Fly

How to Play Vex Try to Fly

Take to the skies in Vex Try to Fly! Our iconic stickman hero leaves the ground behind, strapping on experimental wings, gliders, jetpacks, and rocket boosters.

Launch off massive ski ramps, catch thermal updrafts, steer past aerial obstacles, and deploy emergency parachutes for pinpoint landings. Upgrade your flight gear, boost capacity, and aerodynamics with coins earned from each flight.

Soar higher, fly further, and conquer the clouds in Vex Try to Fly!

Controls Guide:

  • Steer / Pitch: A / D or Left / Right Arrows (Balance nose up / down)
  • Boost / Rocket: Spacebar or W / Up Arrow
  • Deploy Parachute / Glider: Down Arrow / S

Gameplay Tips:

  • Keep your flight angle slightly above the horizon to maximize glide distance.
  • Feather the rocket booster in short bursts instead of draining fuel all at once.
  • Target airborne coins and balloon boosts to refuel your jetpack in mid-air.
  • Upgrade glider wings first to dramatically increase passive lift efficiency.
