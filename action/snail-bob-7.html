Snail Bob 7: Fantasy Story

How to Play Snail Bob 7: Fantasy Story

Journey into a mythical fairy-tale realm in Snail Bob 7: Fantasy Story! After reading fantasy books before bedtime, Bob falls asleep and wakes up as an armored snail knight in a world of dragons and magic.

Battle fiery fire-breathing dragons, mischievous goblins, ghost specters, and wizard traps. Cast spells, lower drawbridges, climb castle towers, and dodge dungeon spikes to claim your victory as the realm's greatest hero.

Immerse yourself in legendary medieval humor, epic boss battles, and ingenious brain-teasers!

Controls Guide:

  • Mouse Click: Interact with catapults, drawbridges, and magic spells.
  • Spacebar: Retract into Bob's knight shell to deflect attacks.
  • Turn / Speed: Manage Bob's movement speed and direction.

Gameplay Tips:

  • Bob's iron shell can protect against minor fire sparks and goblin arrows.
  • Use magic transformation potions to shrink or enlarge Bob to fit through castle grates.
  • Defeat the final dragon by dropping heavy chandeliers when it pauses to roar.
  • Discover the 3 mythical stars hidden behind tapestries and magical crystal orbs.
