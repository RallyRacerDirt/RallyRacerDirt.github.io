How to Play Vex 5

Experience the fifth installment of the acclaimed stickman parkour series in Vex 5! Packed with breathtaking visual polish, new hazard mechanics, and thrilling sound design.

Swing across massive gaps on zip lines, jump through gravity-defying portals, dodge laser cannons, and navigate treacherous conveyor belts. Complete Acts with gold, silver, or bronze medals based on your clear times and death counts.

Take on the daunting Challenge Room and show the world your unmatched parkour mastery!

Controls Guide:

  • Movement: Arrow Keys or WASD
  • Wall Jump: Press Up / W while sliding down walls
  • Slide: Press Down / S while sprinting

Gameplay Tips:

  • Grab onto zip lines by jumping into them; let go at the apex of swing for maximum distance.
  • Look ahead for red laser timers to time your dash between beam pulses.
  • Sliding under spikes gives you a lower hitbox than regular crouching.
  • Complete achievements in the main menu to unlock awesome custom stickman skins.
