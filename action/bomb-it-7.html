How to Play Bomb It 7

Step up to the ultimate bomber challenge in Bomb It 7! Complete engaging daily missions, earn stars, and unlock an extensive closet of outfits and accessories for your favorite heroes.

Battle through diverse theme parks, candy lands, and futuristic cities. Utilize advanced super-weapons like laser beams, thunder strikes, and remote bombs to catch unsuspecting rivals off guard.

Whether playing solo or going head-to-head with a companion, Bomb It 7 delivers nonstop smiles, explosions, and tactical puzzle fun!

Controls Guide:

  • Player 1: WASD keys to walk, Spacebar to bomb / fire item
  • Player 2: Arrow keys to walk, Enter key to bomb / fire item

Gameplay Tips:

  • Complete the daily bonus objectives to accumulate coins rapidly.
  • Combine speed buffs with remote detonators for surgical enemy eliminations.
  • Watch out for bouncy springs and teleport pads that can unexpectedly redirect blasts.
  • Equip shields before rushing crowded central item drops.
