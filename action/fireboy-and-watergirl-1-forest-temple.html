How to Play Fireboy and Watergirl 1: Forest Temple

Embark on the original cooperative puzzle adventure in Fireboy and Watergirl 1: Forest Temple! Control both elemental characters simultaneously or team up with a partner to navigate treacherous temple chambers.

Each character possesses distinct elemental strengths: Fireboy can safely stride across sizzling lava lakes, while Watergirl effortlessly wades through pristine water pools. However, both must avoid toxic green muck at all costs!

Coordinate buttons, push heavy stone blocks, pull ancient levers, and collect sparkling red and blue diamonds to unlock the exit doors and achieve record time ranks.
