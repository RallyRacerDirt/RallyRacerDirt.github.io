How to Play Vex 9

Welcome to the future of stickman action in Vex 9! Featuring neon-drenched cyberspace aesthetics, brand new cyber-traps, and ultra-fluid acrobatic controls.

Dash across light bridges, dodge holographic laser nets, and ride high-speed data streams. Precision wall-sliding, gravity inversions, and lightning reaction times are required to survive every cybernetic Act.

Compete against global leaderboards and prove you have what it takes to master Vex 9!

Controls Guide:

  • Navigate: WASD / Arrow Keys
  • Slide & Crouch: S / Down Arrow
  • Air Dash: Double tap direction key in mid-air (when unlocked)

Gameplay Tips:

  • Use holographic data streams to shoot across wide gaps at supersonic speeds.
  • Timing your air dash right before landing cushions high falls and prevents stagger.
  • Deactivate security turrets by hitting control terminals hidden on upper rafters.
