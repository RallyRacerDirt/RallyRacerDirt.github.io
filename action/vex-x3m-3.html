How to Play Vex X3M 3

The pinnacle of extreme stunt racing arrives with Vex X3M 3! Push both rider and machine to the absolute limit across breathtaking high-tech stunt tracks.

Conquer sonic speed loops, anti-gravity ceiling rides, laser gauntlets, and massive volcanic canyon leaps. Show off your stunt repertoire with quadruple flips, precision balance landings, and nitro-fueled rocket speedruns.

Prove you are the undisputed champion of the Vex X3M trilogy!

Controls Guide:

  • Gas / Throttle: W or Up Arrow
  • Brake: S or Down Arrow
  • Air Control: A / D or Left / Right to tilt and perform flips
  • Turbo: Shift or Spacebar

Gameplay Tips:

  • Ride ceiling magnetic tracks by maintaining full speed through loop entry points.
  • Chain multiple flips on mega-ramps to shave seconds off your completion clock.
  • Release the gas momentarily before landing on bouncy cushions to avoid excessive recoil.
  • Compete for the gold medal on every level to unlock the legendary secret hyperbike!
