How to Play Fireboy and Watergirl 6: Fairy Tales

Enter an enchanted magical realm in Fireboy and Watergirl 6: Fairy Tales! In this whimsical installment, glowing fairies join your quest to help unlock ancient mystical doors and gears.

Use your mouse to drag and direct radiant fairies to power generators, turn wheel mechanisms, and illuminate dark pathways. Meanwhile, control Fireboy and Watergirl to jump across perilous gaps, avoid toxic ooze, and collect diamonds.

A fresh blend of mouse manipulation and keyboard platforming makes this chapter uniquely delightful for solo or duo play!
