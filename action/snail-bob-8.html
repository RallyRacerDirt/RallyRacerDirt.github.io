Snail Bob 8: Island Story

How to Play Snail Bob 8: Island Story

Stranded in tropical paradise in Snail Bob 8: Island Story! While ice fishing with Grandpa, Bob gets separated on an ice floe and drifts ashore onto a remote, mysterious jungle island.

Help Bob navigate through tropical rainforests, sandy beaches, bubbling lava calderas, and ancient tribal villages. Avoid carnivorous piranhas, tribal snail traps, spitting tiki totems, and treacherous rope bridges.

Solve dynamic jungle puzzles, collect hidden stars, and help Snail Bob build a radio to call for rescue!

Controls Guide:

  • Mouse: Click levers, jungle vines, and island devices.
  • Spacebar: Stop and hide inside Bob's shell.
  • Speed & Turn Controls: Control Bob's traversal pace.

Gameplay Tips:

  • Cut hanging vines with your cursor to drop coconuts onto sleeping tribal sentries.
  • Avoid touching volcanic hot springs or carnivorous jungle plants.
  • Look behind tropical ferns and hollow tiki statues to uncover all 3 stars.
  • Time Bob's movement across rising geysers to launch him up to high cliff ledges.
