How to Play Fireboy and Watergirl 7: And Friends

Fireboy and Watergirl 7: And Friends expands the beloved universe with all-new elemental companions and mind-bending puzzle challenges! Explore magical shrines filled with intricate contraptions, light beams, and gravity switches.

Work synchronously with your partner to manipulate complex machinery, align reflective mirrors, and unlock synchronized doorways. Every stage challenges your team's communication, timing, and problem-solving ingenuity.

Experience fresh level mechanics, beautifully rendered puzzle chambers, and the timeless thrill of cooperative teamwork.

Controls Guide:

  • Fireboy: Arrow Keys (Up, Left, Right)
  • Watergirl: W, A, D Keys (W = Jump, A/D = Move)
  • Friends / Allies: Controlled via dedicated keybinds or teamwork cooperation.

Gameplay Tips:

  • Communicate closely with your co-op partner to coordinate simultaneous button activations.
  • Use moving platforms to transport your partner across wide hazard gaps safely.
  • Look for hidden switches that reveal secret diamond paths.
  • Replay levels to optimize your run time and claim the highest ranking stars.
