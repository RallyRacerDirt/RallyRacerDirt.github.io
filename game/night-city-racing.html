Night City Racing

How to Play Night City Racing

Night City Racing takes you into the neon-lit streets of a futuristic city where fast cars and high stakes meet! Race through the bustling city at night, weaving between traffic and avoiding obstacles as you try to reach the finish line before your opponents. The game combines thrilling street racing with a visually stunning urban environment.

Choose from a variety of high-performance cars, each with unique attributes and tuning options. Customize your ride for maximum speed, handling, and acceleration. The city’s vibrant nightlife and challenging courses offer the perfect backdrop for adrenaline-pumping races, filled with sharp turns and high-speed straights.

Whether you’re racing against AI rivals or competing in multiplayer mode, Night City Racing offers intense racing action in a futuristic setting. Can you dominate the neon streets and become the fastest driver in the city?

Controls Guide:

  • ↑ - Accelerate
  • ↓ - Brake/Reverse
  • ← - Steer Left
  • → - Steer Right
  • Space - Handbrake
  • Shift - Nitro Boost

Racing Tips:

  • Use the handbrake for sharp turns to maintain control at high speeds.
  • Save your nitro boost for straightaways or overtaking opponents.
  • Master the drift technique to navigate tight corners more effectively.
  • Keep an eye on your minimap to anticipate sharp turns and obstacles.
