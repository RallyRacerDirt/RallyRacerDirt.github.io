Highway Racer 2

How to Play Highway Racer 2

Highway Racer 2 is an adrenaline-filled racing game where you navigate busy highways, weaving through traffic at high speeds. Test your reflexes and driving skills as you dodge vehicles, overtake opponents, and aim for the highest scores in endless, time trial, or competitive modes.

Choose from a wide selection of cars, each with customizable performance and appearance. Earn points and money by driving dangerously close to other vehicles, maintaining high speeds, and completing objectives. The game features stunning graphics, dynamic weather conditions, and multiple highway environments to keep every race exciting.

Whether you're chasing high scores or competing against rivals, Highway Racer 2 offers an exhilarating driving experience for racing enthusiasts!

Controls Guide:

  • ↑ - Accelerate
  • ↓ - Brake/Reverse
  • ← - Steer Left
  • → - Steer Right
  • Shift - Nitro Boost

Driving Tips:

  • Drive close to other vehicles at high speeds to earn bonus points.
  • Use the nitro boost on straight roads to maximize speed.
  • Avoid collisions to maintain your streak and high score multiplier.
  • Upgrade your car’s engine, brakes, and handling for better performance.
  • Master different weather conditions and adjust your driving style accordingly.
