Traffic Tour

How to Play Traffic Tour

Traffic Tour is an exciting and fast-paced driving game where you navigate through busy city streets, highways, and scenic routes while avoiding traffic and reaching your destination. The goal is to drive as far as possible without crashing into other vehicles, all while completing missions and collecting rewards.

With smooth controls, various weather conditions, and a variety of vehicles to choose from, Traffic Tour keeps you on the edge of your seat as you race against time and traffic. The game features multiple game modes, including endless racing, missions, and time trials, offering something for every type of player.

Unlock new cars, customize your ride, and climb the leaderboards to show off your skills. Can you handle the rush of traffic and become the ultimate driver?

Controls Guide:

  • ↑ - Accelerate
  • ↓ - Brake/Reverse
  • ← - Steer Left
  • → - Steer Right
  • Space - Use Horn

Driving Tips:

  • Stay in the center lane for smoother driving and fewer obstacles.
  • Use your horn to alert other vehicles when necessary.
  • Switch lanes carefully to avoid collisions with other cars.
  • Complete missions to earn extra rewards and unlock new cars.
