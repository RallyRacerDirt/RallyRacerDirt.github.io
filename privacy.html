1. Introduction

Welcome to Rally Racer Dirt (accessible at https://rallyracerdirt.github.io). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we utilize that information, and what rights you hold concerning your digital footprint.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. When you play games on Rally Racer Dirt, we may collect:

  • Device and Browser Data: Screen resolution, browser type, operating system, and language settings to render games at optimal resolution.
  • Local Storage Data: High scores, unlocked cars, and game progress stored locally in your browser. This information never leaves your device.
  • Log & Analytics Information: IP address, referring URL, time spent per game, and pages viewed to help us troubleshoot technical glitches and improve game offerings.

3. Advertising and Cookies

To keep all our games 100% free, we partner with advertising networks such as Google AdSense. These networks may use cookies or web beacons to display relevant advertisements:

  • Google and third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other internet sites.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also manage or disable cookies at any time directly through your web browser preferences.

4. Third-Party Game Developers

Certain games hosted on our platform are provided by licensed third-party developers or platforms. While we vet all games for security and cleanliness, these external game engines may interact with analytics SDKs according to their own privacy standards.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Rally Racer Dirt provides family-safe entertainment. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under regulations like the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you are entitled to:

  • Request access to any personal data held about you.
  • Request rectification or deletion of your stored records.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data.
  • Receive prompt responses to any privacy concerns without discrimination.

7. Contact Our Privacy Team

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Center or our data practices, please reach out via our Contact Us page or email us directly at privacy@rallyracerdirt.github.io.
